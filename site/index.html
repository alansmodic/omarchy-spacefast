    2three ways in
    
Wherever your hands already are.

    
Three small pieces that share one little command, omarchy-spacefast. Install all of them, or just the one that fits how you work.

Share → Web

      
A new Web entry right next to LocalSend in the Omarchy menu. Pick a file, folder, or the clipboard. Clipboard images become an image page, HTML is published as-is, and text becomes a readable page.

An SF in your bar

      
Click for your spaces: open one, copy a share link, or claim it before it expires (it turns red in its last 6 hours). Right-click publishes a folder. Publishing is click-only, so a stray keystroke never sends anything anywhere.

    3how it ships
    
One command. Zero ceremony.

    
Spacefast turns a folder into a live URL, with versions, share links, and private-by-default spaces. omarchy-spacefast handles the rest: it picks the best available engine, remembers where each path went, and cleans up after itself.

  

  

    

      01 · pick
Anything

      
./dist folderreport.html fileclipboard text · img

      
Secrets stay home: .env*, .git, and node_modules are never uploaded.

    

    

      02 · engine
Best available

      
sf spacefast CLInpx spacefast if Nodecurl always works

      
Only needs jq and curl, which Omarchy already ships.

    

    

      03 · version
Updates, not duplicates

      
v1
v2
v3 · live

      
Publish the same path again and the same space gets a new immutable version. Use --new to fork.

    

    

      04 · share
Link in hand

      
⎘link copied to clipboard

      
Plus a desktop notification, and the bar widget refreshes itself.

    4themes
    
It wears your theme. Mostly.

    
The bar icon is Spacefast's SF mark, drawn natively in QML so it stays crisp at bar size. The letters follow your Omarchy theme and the lime offset stays, because some things shouldn't change. (Set brandAccent: false if you disagree.) Pick a theme and this whole page follows.

Omarchy-shapedLives in the menu you already use, the bar you already look at, and the terminal you already live in.

    
Safe by defaultSpaces start private. Claim keys are stored mode 600 and never printed. Publishing from the panel is click-only.

    
Plain and inspectableOne bash script, jq, and curl. No daemon. The uninstaller removes exactly what the installer added.

    
Real hostingSpacefast runs on WordPress.com infrastructure, with versions, share links, custom domains, and more once you sign in.
